## Belt Chain
printed alternative for the Z chain that uses a length of gt2 belt down the center of it 

<img width="558" height="796" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/d6b56840-f28f-4702-b451-2d8b436647db" />
<img width="457" height="729" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/20e0d245-f581-4fcc-9cc1-5d1a0d27cd45" />

to secure the belt to the chain ends , a short piece of filament is pressed into the printed part between the teeth of the belt
<img width="582" height="694" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/97e89920-0789-4525-a6ac-ad8708978521" />

then you can thread each link down onto the belt one at a time 
<img width="694" height="769" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/a625b831-f055-41c6-97cd-07543689262a" />

then secure the other end the same way you did the first end 

this works with stock [Micron 2 hole chain mounts](https://github.com/PrintersForAnts/Micron/tree/main/STLs/Z_Chain/2_hole_chain)
